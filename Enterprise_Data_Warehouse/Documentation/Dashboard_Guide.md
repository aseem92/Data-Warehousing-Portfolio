# Dashboard Guide

## Executive Overview

This dashboard provides high-level business performance metrics.

### KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

### Visuals

- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Branch
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Segment

---

## Sales Analytics

This dashboard focuses on sales performance across different business dimensions.

### Visuals

- Revenue by State
- Revenue by City
- Employee Performance
- Top Customers
- Revenue vs Profit by Branch

---

## Profit Insights

This dashboard analyzes profitability trends.

### Visuals

- Monthly Revenue vs Profit
- Branch Profitability
- Product Category Performance
- Customer Profitability
- Profit Trend Analysis

---

## Business Value

The dashboards enable management to:

- Monitor KPIs
- Identify top-performing branches
- Track customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze employee performance
- Improve profitability
- Support strategic decision-making
